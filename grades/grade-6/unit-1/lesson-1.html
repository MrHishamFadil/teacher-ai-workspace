Lesson 1: Future Goals

Grade: 6   |   Unit: 1   |   Status: Teacher review sample

Lesson plan

Warm-up

Students discuss goals they can achieve this school year and choose one personal target.

Teach

Model future forms with will and going to using clear classroom examples.

Practice

Students sort future sentences into plans, predictions, and promises.

Review

Students write a short goal statement and one action step.

Learning objectives

  • Talk about future plans and goals.
  • Use will and going to in simple sentences.
  • Write a short paragraph about one learning goal.

Vocabulary and grammar

Vocabulary: goal, plan, future, career, prepare, achieve.

Grammar focus: Future forms with will and going to.

Practice questions

  1. Complete: I am going to ______ for my test.
  2. Write one sentence with will.
  3. Write one goal for this month.
  4. Explain one step that can help you achieve your goal.

Student checklist

  • I can explain one future goal.
  • I used will or going to correctly.
  • I wrote one clear action step.

Parent note

Ask your child to share one school goal and explain how they will work toward it this week.
