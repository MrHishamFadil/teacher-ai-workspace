Lesson 1: Healthy Habits

Grade: 4   |   Unit: 1   |   Status: Teacher review sample

Lesson plan

Warm-up

Students list healthy actions they already know, such as drinking water or sleeping early.

Teach

Introduce adverbs of frequency and model where they go in short sentences.

Practice

Students complete sentences using always, sometimes, and never.

Review

Students write two healthy habit sentences and share one with a partner.

Learning objectives

  • Talk about daily habits.
  • Use adverbs of frequency in short sentences.
  • Write advice about one healthy routine.

Vocabulary and grammar

Vocabulary: healthy, exercise, water, sleep, sometimes, always.

Grammar focus: Adverbs of frequency before the main verb.

Practice questions

  1. Complete: I ______ drink water after exercise.
  2. Write one sentence with sometimes.
  3. Choose the healthy habit: sleep early / skip breakfast.
  4. Write one advice sentence for a friend.

Student checklist

  • I can name three healthy habits.
  • I used an adverb of frequency correctly.
  • I wrote one advice sentence.

Parent note

Ask your child to describe one healthy habit they do every day and one habit they want to improve.
