Lesson 1: My School Day

Grade: 3   |   Unit: 1   |   Status: Teacher review sample

Lesson plan

Warm-up

Students name three things they use at school and say one classroom routine.

Teach

Introduce school vocabulary with pictures, gestures, and short teacher examples.

Practice

Students complete oral sentence frames: I read a book. We write words.

Review

Students choose two words and use each word in a simple sentence.

Learning objectives

  • Name common classroom routines.
  • Use simple present sentences about school.
  • Read and write short school-day sentences.

Vocabulary and grammar

Vocabulary: classroom, teacher, book, pencil, read, write.

Grammar focus: Simple present with I and we.

Practice questions

  1. Write one sentence with book.
  2. Complete: I ______ with a pencil.
  3. Circle the school word: pencil, apple, chair.
  4. Write one sentence that begins with We.

Student checklist

  • I can say the vocabulary words.
  • I can write one school sentence.
  • I checked capital letters and full stops.

Parent note

Ask your child to point to school items at home and say one simple sentence about each item.
