Lesson 1: Learning Together

Grade: 5   |   Unit: 1   |   Status: Teacher review sample

Learning objectives

  • Identify vocabulary related to teamwork and classroom learning.
  • Use the simple present tense to describe routines and habits.
  • Write short sentences about how students can learn together.

Key vocabulary

teamwork, partner, explain, listen, share, improve, question, example

Grammar focus

Simple present tense: Use the base verb for I, you, we, and they. Add -s or -es for he, she, and it.

Examples: We share ideas. She explains the answer.

Short teacher explanation

Good learners do not work alone all the time. They ask questions, listen carefully, and explain their ideas. When students work with a partner, they can compare answers and learn from mistakes.

In this lesson, students practise words about teamwork and use the simple present tense to describe helpful classroom habits.

Practice questions

  1. Write one sentence with the word partner.
  2. Choose the correct verb: She share / shares her idea.
  3. Write two classroom habits that help students learn.
  4. Complete the sentence: We ______ carefully when a friend explains.
  5. Write a short sentence about teamwork.

Student self-study checklist

  • I read the vocabulary words aloud.
  • I understood the simple present examples.
  • I answered the practice questions.
  • I asked for help if a word was difficult.

Parent note

Please ask your child to explain one new word and read one sentence aloud. Praise effort and clear explanation more than perfect answers.
