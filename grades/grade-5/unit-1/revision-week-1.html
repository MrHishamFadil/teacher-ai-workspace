Weekly Revision: Grade 5 Unit 1 Week 1

A teacher-reviewed revision sample covering vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing, and a mini quiz.

Lessons covered

  • Lesson 1: Learning Together
  • Focus skill: Simple present tense for routines and habits

Vocabulary review

Match each word to its meaning: teamwork, partner, explain, improve.

  1. To make something better
  2. A person who works with you
  3. To make an idea clear
  4. Working well with others

Grammar review

Choose the correct simple present verb.

  1. He listen / listens carefully.
  2. They share / shares examples.
  3. She ask / asks a useful question.

Reading practice

Mona and Ali study together after school. Mona reads the question first. Ali explains his idea. They compare answers and correct one mistake. At the end, both students understand the lesson better.

  1. Who reads the question first?
  2. What do Mona and Ali compare?
  3. Why is studying together helpful?

Writing task

Write 5 sentences about how a good student learns with a partner. Use at least two vocabulary words from the lesson.

Mini quiz

  1. Write the third-person form of explain.
  2. Complete: We ______ our ideas in class.
  3. Circle the teamwork word: apple, partner, window.
  4. Write one sentence with improve.

Answer key

Vocabulary: 1 improve, 2 partner, 3 explain, 4 teamwork.

Grammar: 1 listens, 2 share, 3 asks.

Reading: 1 Mona. 2 They compare answers. 3 It helps students explain, correct mistakes, and understand better.

Mini quiz: 1 explains. 2 share. 3 partner. 4 Answers will vary.
