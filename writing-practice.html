Writing Practice

A simple writing task, rubric, checklist, and teacher feedback categories for safe manual review.

Writing task

Topic: Write a short paragraph about a place where students can learn something new.

Write 6 to 8 sentences. Include a topic sentence, three clear details, and a closing sentence.

Simple writing rubric

Skill What to check
Ideas The paragraph answers the topic and includes clear details.
Organization The paragraph has a beginning, middle, and ending.
Grammar Sentences use correct punctuation and simple verb forms.
Vocabulary The student uses useful describing words.

Future automation placeholder

Later, students may submit writing through Google Forms. A private Google Sheet could collect responses, draft teacher feedback, and wait for teacher approval before anything is shared.

This MVP does not collect submissions, store student data, or send automated feedback.

Student checklist

  • I wrote one clear topic sentence.
  • I added at least three details.
  • I checked capital letters and full stops.
  • I used at least two describing words.
  • I read my paragraph aloud once.

Teacher feedback categories

  • Strong idea
  • Clearer sentence needed
  • Grammar check
  • Add more detail
  • Good vocabulary choice
