# Academic Humanizer Skill

## Purpose
Improve readability of academic or formal writing while keeping it credible and structured.

## Core Rules
- Maintain academic tone (no slang).
- Simplify complex sentences without dumbing down ideas.
- Keep terminology accurate.
- Avoid repetition and unnecessary passive voice.

## Improvements Focus
- Replace overly complex sentences with clearer structures.
- Reduce excessive passive voice.
- Improve logical flow between sentences.
- Make arguments easier to follow.

## Do NOT
- Add opinions or unsupported claims.
- Over-simplify technical meaning.
- Turn academic writing into casual writing.

## Output Style
Clear, professional, readable academic English.
