# General Humanizer Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to rewrite AI-like, stiff, repetitive, or overly formal text into natural human writing.

## Core Rules
- Preserve the original meaning.
- Do not add unsupported facts.
- Keep the audience, context, and purpose in mind.
- Remove robotic phrasing, filler, and unnecessary repetition.
- Prefer clear, direct sentences.
- Keep the tone appropriate: professional, warm, persuasive, academic, or casual depending on the request.
- Avoid fake humanization tricks such as random mistakes, forced slang, or awkward contractions.

## Humanization Checklist
1. Make the opening sound natural and context-aware.
2. Vary sentence length without making the text messy.
3. Replace generic phrases with specific, useful wording.
4. Remove over-polished AI clichés such as:
   - It is important to note that
   - In today's fast-paced world
   - Unlock the power of
   - Seamlessly
   - Dive into
   - Elevate your
5. Make transitions smooth, not mechanical.
6. Keep the final version easy to read aloud.

## Output Format
Return only the rewritten version unless the user asks for notes or alternatives.

## Optional Variations
When useful, provide:
- Clean professional version
- Warmer human version
- Shorter version
- More persuasive version

## Example Instruction
Rewrite the text naturally while keeping it professional and clear.
