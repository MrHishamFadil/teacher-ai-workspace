# Persuasive Humanizer Skill

## Purpose
Turn neutral or robotic text into engaging and convincing human writing.

## Core Rules
- Keep meaning intact.
- Add clarity, flow, and emotional connection.
- Focus on reader benefit.

## Techniques
- Use strong but natural hooks.
- Make sentences more conversational (without being informal).
- Highlight value clearly.
- Improve rhythm and readability.

## Avoid
- Overhype
- Fake urgency
- Clickbait tone

## Output Style
Engaging, clear, confident, and human.
