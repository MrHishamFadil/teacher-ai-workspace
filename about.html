MH

Teacher profile

Mr. Hisham Fadil

Mr. Hisham Fadil has over 20 years of teaching experience. He began his career in Egypt and has been teaching at international schools in Saudi Arabia since 2015. He specialises in English Language Arts for Grades 3-6, following the Connect Plus and Savvas Realize American curricula.

Before returning to teaching full-time, he led teams at Vodafone UK offshore and Microsoft Xbox UK as a Team Manager, which gives him a unique understanding of communication, structure, and professional development. He created this platform to give his students a reliable, curriculum-aligned home for English learning.
